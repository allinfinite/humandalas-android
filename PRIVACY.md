# Humandalas Privacy

Humandalas 2.0.1 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app works offline. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, notification, or internet permission. It contains no analytics, advertising, push-notification, billing, login, tracking, or cloud service.

Choosing “Share this card” opens Android's system share chooser. Humandalas does not learn which app you choose or receive the shared content back. The selected receiving app has its own privacy practices.

Android or the device manufacturer may provide operating-system backup or device-transfer services for application state. This release does not currently save user-created content or an account.

Effective: September 21, 2026.

Privacy questions may be submitted through the public source repository issue tracker.
