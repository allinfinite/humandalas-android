Humandalas is a simple group practice for gathering with intention.

Build a complete Humandala in five stages: Connection, Intention, Cultivation, Offering, and Anchor. The guided flow selects one practice from each stage, or you can browse the complete 46-card library at your own pace.

Everything works offline. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, purchases, or tracking.
